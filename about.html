About Sunrise Bake House

Sunrise Bake House is a fictional family bakery created for this learning project. We started with one simple idea: fresh baking should be affordable and available to everyone in Thane.

Our Values

  • Fresh ingredients, baked daily
  • Clear prices, no hidden charges
  • Everyone is welcome, accessible shop and website
